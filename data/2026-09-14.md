### [1] [FoldNet++: a Large-Scale Synthetic Dataset for Robotic T-Shirt Folding and Unfolding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12433)
*Yuxing Chen,Zhiyuan Wei,Bowen Xiao,Zhizheng Zhang,He Wang*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 本文提出一个大规模合成数据集，用于机器人T恤折叠与展开任务，覆盖6种机器人本体、1K件T恤、1K个环境资产和120K条带丰富标注的episode，仅用合成数据训练的模型可直接迁移到真实环境，端到端任务成功率超90%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 衣物高度可变形的特性使得训练具有泛化能力的机器人T恤折叠与展开策略仍然是一个重大挑战，缺乏大规模、多样化的训练数据是主要瓶颈。

Method: 首先沿用FoldNet流程生成大规模可物理仿真的T恤数据集，具有多样外观和标注的语义关键点；然后基于这些语义关键点，通过统一的规则化框架为不同机器人本体生成操作演示；再利用这些演示训练视觉运动策略。

Result: 实验结果表明，仅在本合成数据上训练的模型，直接部署到未见过的真实环境和任意初始构型的未见T恤时，端到端任务成功率可超过90%。

Conclusion: 该大规模合成数据集可支持训练多种操作策略，并展现出从合成数据到真实世界场景的有效零样本迁移能力。

Abstract: Due to the highly deformable nature of garments, training a generalizable policy for robotic T-shirt folding and unfolding remains a significant challenge. In this work, we present a large-scale synthetic dataset for robotic T-shirt folding and unfolding, covering 6 robotic embodiments, 1K T-shirts, 1K environmental assets, and 120K episodes with rich annotations, which can be used to train a wide range of manipulation policies. We first follow the FoldNet pipeline to generate a large-scale dataset of physically simulatable T-shirts with diverse appearances and annotated semantic keypoints. Based on these semantic keypoints, we then generate manipulation demonstrations for different robotic embodiments through a unified rule-based framework. We use these demonstrations to train visuomotor policies, and experimental results demonstrate that models trained solely on our synthetic data can achieve over 90\% end-to-end task success rates when directly deployed to unseen real-world environments and previously unseen T-shirts from arbitrary initial configurations. Project URL: https://pku-epic.github.io/FoldNetXX/.

### [2] [ProClosure: Hierarchical Room-Object Assignment using Progressive Boundary Closure from Monocular Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12614)
*Vinoth Kumar Muthuraj,Soumyadeep Banik,Kushal Sharma,Hardik Jain*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 提出渐进边界闭合方法，从单目RGB视频恢复房间层，用于3D场景图的房间划分与物体归属。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 3D场景图将物体归入房间，是机器人按房间名取物的依据；物体若被记入错误房间则无法检索。难点在于俯视地图中墙体仅记录于相机看过处，边界缺口无法区分门洞与未观测墙段，先前方法将两者都当通道，导致本应分开的房间被合并。

Method: 用SLAM前端与开放词汇分割器得到结构点云、相机轨迹与物体轨迹；点云栅格化为俯视地图。观察到门洞与未观测缺口都需封闭且无需区分：开口在少量边界增长下闭合，且少有视线穿过，故用前者恢复房间、后者分配物体。房间通过对边界逐步向内增厚、将自由空间区域在封闭时冻结获得，使每个开口按自身尺度封闭；以相机位姿为种子，去除采样启发式，使分割确定。

Result: 在6个HM3D-Semantics场景的10层楼上，于相同俯视地图上与HOV-SG对比：恢复74个房间对应72个标注区域（HOV-SG为44），IoU 0.25下房间F_1从0.741提升至0.890（精度略有代价），物体到房间ARI从0.488提升至0.696（p=0.002，每层均领先）。

Conclusion: 渐进边界闭合能有效恢复房间层并提升房间分割与物体归属精度，且无需区分门洞与未观测缺口，方法确定性强。

Abstract: A 3D scene graph groups objects into rooms. When a robot is asked to fetch an object from the kitchen, that grouping is what tells it where to look. An object recorded in the wrong room is not retrievable by a query naming the correct room. We introduce Progressive Boundary Closure, which recovers room layer from a monocular RGB video. A SLAM front end and an open-vocabulary segmenter supply a structural point cloud, camera trajectory and object tracks. The cloud is rasterised into a top-down map, rooms are recovered from it, and each object takes the room holding most of its extent. The difficulty lies in the map itself. Walls are recorded only where the camera looked, so a gap in the boundary may be a doorway or a stretch of wall that was never observed; nothing distinguishes the two. Prior methods treat both as passages, merging rooms that should remain separate. We observe that both require the same treatment: a room should not extend across either, so both are closed and need not be distinguished. Such an opening closes under a small amount of boundary growth, and few sightlines cross it, so points in different rooms rarely see one another. We use the first to recover rooms and the second to assign objects to them. Rooms are obtained by Progressively thickening the boundary inward and freezing each free-space region once it becomes enclosed, so every opening seals at its own scale rather than at a radius fixed in advance. Camera poses are used as seeds, which removes the sampling heuristic and makes the segmentation deterministic. Over 10 floors of 6 HM3D-Semantics scenes, scored against HOV-SG on identical top-down maps, we recover 74 rooms for 72 annotated regions (HOV-SG: 44), raising room F_1 from 0.741 to 0.890 at IoU 0.25 at some cost in precision, and object-to-room ARI from 0.488 to 0.696 (p=0.002, ahead on every floor).

### [3] [Global Path Planner with Multi-Model Switching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13015)
*Pietro Gori,Francesco Iotti,Eduard Zelenay,Rastislav Marko,Michele Pierallini,Franco Angelini,Gabriele Pannocchia,Manolo Garabini*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 本文提出一种结合多模型运动学切换的纯追踪控制器，用于增强全局路径规划在不同地形下的保真度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 标准路径规划与跟踪在多样地形下难以保持规划一致性，需要根据地形特征和机器人状态自适应调整运动学模型。

Method: 系统包含三部分：用于地形分析的可通行性图、用于生成可行路径的Heading-Aware A*算法，以及用于动态跟踪的多模型纯追踪控制器。核心创新是自适应运动学建模，可基于地形特征与机器人状态实时切换运动学模型。

Result: 在Artaban四足机器人和X3四旋翼无人机两种平台上进行仿真验证，相比标准基线在性能、鲁棒性和适应性上均有提升。

Conclusion: 该方法通过自适应运动学切换优化了复杂场景下的路径效率与能耗，具备良好的通用性与适应性。

Abstract: This work enhances global path planning via a pure-pursuit controller with multi-model kinematic switching that sustains plan fidelity across diverse terrains. The system includes a traversability graph for terrain analysis, a Heading-Aware A* algorithm for generating feasible paths, and a multi-model Pure Pursuit controller for dynamic tracking. A core innovation is adaptive kinematic modeling, enabling real-time switching between kinematic models based on terrain features and robot states. This adaptability optimizes path efficiency and energy use in challenging scenarios. We validate the approach in simulation on different platforms, namely the Artaban quadruped and the X3 quadrotor drone, showcasing improved performance, robustness, and adaptability over standard baselines.

</details>
